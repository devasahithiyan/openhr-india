Welcome to Kaveri Textiles

We make cotton and blended fabrics in Coimbatore and sell them from our offices in Bengaluru and Pune. This handbook covers what you need in your first week.

Your first day

Come to your branch reception at the time in your first-week schedule. Your buddy will show you around, and your manager will walk you through the rest of the week.

Equipment and access

HR prepares your laptop, ID card and, in Coimbatore, your factory safety kit. If anything is missing, raise a blocker in My onboarding and HR will record the handover and resolution there.

Leave, attendance and pay

Punch in and out each working day. Apply for leave in the app; your branch's holidays and weekly offs are not counted. Salaries are paid on the last working day of the month.
